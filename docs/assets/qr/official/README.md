# K-DRUM Official Website QR Assets

Reusable official QR assets for presentations, reports, posters, and external sharing.

**Encoded URL:** https://youngteckhur.github.io/KDRUM-Public/

## Canonical files

- `KDRUM_QR_Standalone_Vector.svg` — standalone QR for general use
- `KDRUM_QR_PPT_Card.svg` — presentation card
- `KDRUM_QR_Print_Report.svg` — report and print card

## Recommended use

SVG is the canonical format in this public-information repository. It remains sharp at any size and can be inserted directly into recent versions of PowerPoint, Word, Keynote, and most design software. Export to PNG only when a destination specifically requires a raster image.

## QR rule

The QR encodes the official homepage URL directly and does not depend on a third-party dynamic QR or URL-shortening service. The QR itself remains black on white for robust scanning; K-water-like colors are used only in the surrounding card design.
