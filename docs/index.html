K-DRUM

K-water Grid-based Distributed Rainfall rUnoff Model

An established, physically based grid-distributed hydrologic model from K-water, supported by a public research record in flood simulation, long-term hydrology, snowmelt, drought, parallel computation, and surface-water/groundwater coupling.

The current v3.x generation builds on this hydrologic foundation to connect watershed runoff, river networks, floodplains, model preparation, and result interpretation within a progressively integrated platform.

A model built from spatial hydrology outward

K-DRUM's defining foundation is a physically based, grid-distributed representation of watershed response. The current platform extends that foundation toward a continuous hydrologic-hydraulic workflow.

Spatial rainfall-runoff processes

Grid rainfall, infiltration, evapotranspiration, surface runoff, subsurface runoff and spatial water-balance processes.

Event and long-term simulation

Public studies include flood events, long-term runoff, evapotranspiration, snow accumulation and snowmelt applications.

Flow routing and river networks

Established kinematic-wave routing is being complemented by current-generation 1D dynamic-wave river-network hydraulics.

River-floodplain interaction

Current v3.x extensions include two-way 1D-2D exchange and two-dimensional inundation analysis for integrated flood studies.

Terrain and channel geometry

High-resolution terrain, river alignment, cross-section preparation and virtual channel-bed processing support hydraulic model construction.

Preparation and interpretation tools

InputStudio supports project authoring and quality checks; FloodViewer supports spatial and time-varying result interpretation.

The current K-DRUM platform

The current program is organized as a model ecosystem rather than a single executable: preparation, simulation, standardized outputs and interpretation are treated as connected parts of the workflow.

Core and supporting components

  • K-DRUM Core: distributed hydrology and the current hydrologic-hydraulic simulation engine.
  • InputStudio: project configuration, time series, geometry, scenarios and consistency checks.
  • FloodViewer: integrated visualization of spatial and time-dependent simulation results.
  • ChannelBed: reusable high-resolution terrain and channel-geometry processing.

Research extensions

Estuary2DV is maintained as an independent longitudinal-vertical hydrodynamics and salinity research prototype for future controlled coupling with river hydraulics and structure operation.

Research extensions are presented separately from the established hydrologic core so that scope and maturity remain clear.

Evidence, maturity and interpretation

The hydrologic core is established through the public K-DRUM research record. Current hydraulic and platform extensions are being validated progressively and should be interpreted according to their documented maturity.

Objective status boundary. The homepage summarizes what K-DRUM is and why it is technically relevant. Detailed claims about production readiness are maintained separately in the public development-status table and model card.
