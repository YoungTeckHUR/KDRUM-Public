K-DRUM

K-water Grid-based Distributed Rainfall rUnoff Model

A physically based, grid-distributed hydrologic model developed by K-water, with an established public research record in flood runoff, long-term hydrology, snowmelt, drought, parallel computation and surface-water–groundwater studies.

The current v3.x platform builds on that foundation to connect watershed runoff, river networks, floodplains, project authoring and result interpretation in one modeling workflow.

From spatial hydrology to an integrated modeling system

K-DRUM begins with a physically based description of watershed response at grid scale. The current platform extends that foundation toward connected river and floodplain analysis.

Distributed rainfall-runoff

Spatial rainfall, infiltration, evapotranspiration, surface runoff, subsurface runoff and water-balance processes.

Event and long-term hydrology

Flood-event simulation together with published long-term runoff, snowmelt, drought and water-cycle applications.

Flow routing and river hydraulics

Established kinematic routing with current extensions for cross-section-based 1D dynamic-wave river networks.

River-floodplain interaction

Two-way exchange between 1D rivers and 2D floodplain domains for inundation analysis.

Terrain and channel geometry

High-resolution terrain, river alignment, cross-section preparation and virtual channel-bed processing.

Authoring and interpretation

InputStudio for project construction and quality checks; FloodViewer for spatial and time-varying result interpretation.

Research extensions

ChannelBed supports reusable high-resolution channel-geometry processing. Estuary2DV is maintained as a separate research prototype for longitudinal-vertical estuary hydrodynamics and salinity.
