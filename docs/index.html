K-DRUM

K-DRUM is K-water's physically based, grid-based distributed hydrologic model for rainfall-runoff simulation and integrated watershed analysis.

K-DRUM은 K-water에서 개발해 온 물리적 기반의 격자형 분포형 강우-유출 모형이며, 현재 유역 수문·수리 통합 해석 방향으로 확장 개발되고 있습니다.

Public information scope

This site is a public technical-information page for the current K-DRUM development context. It does not replace K-water's official institutional information; K-water/MyWater remains the authoritative source for institutional and official product information.

Not published here: production source code, internal validation datasets, solver tuning details, development branches or commit identifiers, sensitive operational rules or data, failure diagnostics, proprietary basin inputs, and unpublished numerical implementation details.

Established hydrologic background

Published K-DRUM studies describe physically based grid-distributed rainfall-runoff simulation, spatial and radar rainfall, kinematic-wave routing, Green-Ampt infiltration, surface and subsurface runoff, long-term hydrology, snow-related processes, MPI parallelization, drought applications, and surface-water/groundwater coupling.

Current development direction

The current development program extends K-DRUM beyond its historical distributed rainfall-runoff role toward an integrated hydrologic and hydraulic modeling environment.

  • 1D dynamic-wave river-network hydraulics
  • branched and merged river networks
  • bidirectional 1D-2D river-floodplain coupling
  • 2D flood-inundation analysis
  • hydraulic structures and reservoir-operation scenarios
  • high-resolution terrain, river alignment, and virtual channel-bed processing
  • NetCDF-centered integrated outputs and parallel computation
  • K-DRUM InputStudio project authoring
  • K-DRUM FloodViewer result visualization
  • experimental K-DRUM Estuary2DV hydrodynamics and salinity research
